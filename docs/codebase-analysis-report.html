01Executive summary

Why 5 and not 7. Every module-level quality signal this audit can measure is good-to-excellent: zero circular imports across 146 modules, zero TODO/FIXME markers, zero any in non-test source, a domain invariant genuinely enforced in a single IndexedDB transaction, and a verified production CSP. How the findings were produced

Metric collection was performed directly. Four read-only reviewers then worked in parallel over dimension clusters — security and error handling; architecture and maintainability; test and E2E quality; documentation drift, configuration and performance — each required to open and cite files before asserting anything. Their output was reconciled against the direct measurements rather than trusted. That reconciliation mattered in four places, all of which are recorded above rather than quietly smoothed over:

  • CF-7 was raised as High and refuted by running its own falsifier — the live cache headers are correct.
  • Two findings were raised as Critical (TQ-1, TQ-2) and downgraded to High: both are test-integrity problems with no direct user impact, and Critical is reserved here for something that blocks the team or ships harm.
  • AR-4 was raised as a "user-visible correctness issue" and downgraded to Low after checking the import dialog's actual options — the unhandled 'rename' branch is not reachable from any UI path.
  • A seventh dead export was withdrawn: KeyboardManager.unregisterShortcut has five references, four of them in its own test file. Untested-in-production is a milder and different claim than unreferenced, and it is reported as such in MA-3.

The two headline complexity numbers were also produced twice by different methods — an AST parse (98 functions over 40 lines) and an independent brace-matching parser (83) — and the discrepancy is disclosed in §2 rather than resolved by picking the more dramatic figure.
